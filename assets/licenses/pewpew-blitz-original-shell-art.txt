PewPew Blitz original branding and menu artwork

Files: brawler/ui/branding/*.png, brawler/ui/shell/*.png

All of these files are original work of the PewPew Blitz project. They contain no third-party
artwork. Per-file provenance, recorded in asset_src/player-shell/README.md:

- brawler/ui/branding/pewpew-blitz-lockup.png and pewpew-blitz-wordmark.png: original project
  branding. Their source files are not in the repository.
- brawler/ui/shell/logo.png: an AI-assisted refinement (OpenAI image generation) of the original
  project branding, isolated on a transparent background (BRL-0114). The generated original is
  retained outside the repository.
- brawler/ui/shell/panel-identity.png, underline.png, game-selection-background.png:
  code-authored SVG decorations generated by asset_src/player-shell/build_shell_art.py
  (BRL-0114, BRL-0116, BRL-0156, BRL-4jdx).
- brawler/ui/shell/*-overview.png: schematic Practice map overviews generated from the authored
  map geometry by asset_src/player-shell/generate-map-overviews.py.
