PewPew Blitz original fighter weapons

Files: brawler/models/original/weapons/*.glb

These models are original work of the PewPew Blitz project (BRL-q29d). They are generated by the
checked-in procedural Blender builder asset_src/weapons/build_weapons.py (built with Blender
5.2.3 LTS, `just weapon-assets`) and contain no third-party, commissioned, or hand-edited model
data, textures, or images. The builder script was written with AI assistance; the models are its
deterministic output.
asset_src/weapons/README.md records the build command, the weapon contract, and the inventory.

Weapons (one per primary-weapon animation family, selected by
assets/catalogs/fighter_weapons.ron):
- wpn_blaster.glb: one-handed pulse blaster (Pulse Sidearm, Scatter Cannon, Sticky Blomb).
- wpn_sprayer.glb: two-handed heavy sprayer (Spray).
- wpn_lobber.glb: two-handed lobber (Arc Launcher, Splash).
- wpn_blade.glb: two-handed impact blade (Impact Blade).

They replace the CC0 Kenney Blaster Kit blaster-a.glb, which is no longer shipped.
