# Description

# Outcome

Weapon fire reads by side relative to the local viewer: projectiles fired by the viewer and their
teammates are ally blue, projectiles fired by the other team are enemy red, on every team the viewer
can be assigned to. The blue and red are the same observer-relative relation colours already used by
the fighter ground rings, nameplates, toon rims, and outlines. Weapon identity stays readable: each
weapon keeps its own body shape, size, trail length, and elemental pattern; only the team hue
changes. The straight-bolt nucleus and comet wake, the plain/primitive/lobbed projectile body, the
elemental projectile relationship core, the sentry body, and the neutral straight muzzle flash
shell all follow the rule. This is client presentation only over replicated team membership: no
server, protocol, or gameplay-content change.
